# User Rights

The privacy rights available to you depend on your applicable jurisdiction. Noto adapts its disclosures to your country inside the app; this document summarizes the rights generally available.

## Common rights

Depending on your region, you may have some or all of the following rights:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data (use the in-app data export — see [DATA_EXPORT.md](./DATA_EXPORT.md)).
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (edit your profile and content directly in the app).
- **Erasure:** Delete your data (delete items or your whole account — see [DATA_DELETION.md](./DATA_DELETION.md)).
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (ZIP + JSON export).
- **Objection:** Object to certain processing (e.g. analytics — opt out in Settings).
- **Restriction:** Request that processing of your data be limited.
- **Consent withdrawal:** Withdraw consent for optional processing (AI features, analytics, cookies) at any time.
- **Automated decisions:** Rights regarding solely automated decisions, where applicable.
- **Complaint:** Lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

## By region (as implemented in Noto)

### European Economic Area / GDPR
Framework: **GDPR (EU) + national adaptations.** Consent model: **opt-in** (e.g. cookies). Rights include access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction, portability, objection, protection against automated decisions, consent withdrawal, and complaint to your national Data Protection Authority.

### United Kingdom
Framework: **UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018.** Consent model: opt-in. Complaints to the **ICO**.

### California (CCPA / CPRA)
Framework: **CCPA / CPRA.** Consent model: notice. Rights include knowing what is collected, knowing if data is sold/shared (Noto does not sell your data), deletion, opt-out of sale/sharing, limiting sensitive data use, non-discrimination, and authorized-agent requests. Complaints to the **California Attorney General**.

### Brazil (LGPD)
Framework: **LGPD.** Rights include confirming processing, access, correction, anonymization/deletion, portability, information about shared data, and consent revocation. Authority: **ANPD**.

### Canada (PIPEDA)
Framework: **PIPEDA.** Rights include access, challenging accuracy, withdrawing consent, and complaining to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada**.

### Other regions
A local data-protection law applies. You retain access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, and the ability to contact your local authority.

## Exercising your rights

- **Self-service:** Most rights can be exercised directly in the app (export, edit, delete, opt out of analytics, manage consent).
- **Contact:** For anything you cannot do yourself, contact Noto via the in-app **Contact** page. Noto will respond in accordance with applicable law.

## Noto does not sell your data

Noto does not sell personal data to anyone, and does not share personal data with advertising or ad-tracking providers.
