# International Data Transfers

## Where your data may be processed

Noto is hosted on the **Base44** cloud platform, and uses **AI providers** (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic) for AI features. Cloud hosting, storage, backups, and AI processing may be located **outside your country**.

## Database, storage, and backups (Base44)

- Your structured data, uploaded files, and backups are stored in Base44-managed cloud infrastructure.
- The physical location of this infrastructure is managed by Base44 and **may be outside your country**.
- **Safeguards:** transfers rely on appropriate data-protection mechanisms, such as **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** and the platform's data-processing agreement, where applicable.

## AI providers

- When you use an AI feature, the prompt is sent to an AI provider (OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic) whose cloud is typically in the **US and/or EU**.
- **Safeguards:** Noto relies on the provider's data-processing agreement and applicable transfer mechanisms (e.g. SCCs / DPA) — **TO BE VERIFIED** per provider's current terms.

## Email

- Transactional emails are routed through the Base44 email service and the recipient's email provider. Email delivery may cross borders as part of normal internet routing.

## Your country adaptation

Inside the app, Noto detects your country and adapts the displayed legal framework, rights, and data-protection authority accordingly. The list of countries and their applicable frameworks is maintained in Noto's compliance data (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA, or a local standard).

## Verification

- The exact data-center regions for Base44 and each AI provider are **TO BE VERIFIED** against current provider documentation, as cloud providers may change regions over time.
